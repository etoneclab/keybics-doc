Keybics demo

A walkthrough of Keybics on macOS, about 2½ minutes, English voice-over with captions: creating a connection, generating the keys and the initialisation letter, the INI/HIA registration with the bank, confirming the bank’s keys and exporting the connection.

The bank registration (INI/HIA) needs a real EBICS user at a bank and is explained, not performed, in this video. All data shown is example data. Download the video (MP4, 9 MB).
